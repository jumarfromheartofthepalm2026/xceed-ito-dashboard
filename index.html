🎯 Purpose

The Decision Engine replaces gut-feel prioritization with Operations Research (OR) — the same mathematical discipline used by airlines, logistics companies, and investment banks to allocate resources optimally.

At Xceed ITO's stage, every hour of engineer time and every vendor registration decision has an opportunity cost. This engine makes those trade-offs explicit and defensible — to the board, to partners, and to the team.

📊 Scoring Methodology
Score = Σ (dimensioni × weighti)

Each item is scored on 1–10 per dimension. Dimensions have weights that reflect ITO's current priorities (revenue-first, then strategic fit). The Effort / Ease dimension is inverted: 10 = easiest, 1 = hardest — so low effort items score higher.

All weights sum to 1.0. Changing the weights would change the ranking — the weights are stated explicitly so anyone can challenge them.
