🚪 Morocco Entry Strategy — Why It's Different from Gulf
Morocco is not a price war — the local players are already cheap ($25–49/hr). Xceed ITO's competitive advantage here is vendor depth + multi-country entity structure, not cost. The target is nearshore BPO companies and financial services firms in Casablanca/Rabat who also have Gulf operations.
🇲🇺 Mauritius — Market Context
Mauritius is not a primary competitive battleground — it is Xceed ITO's DR-as-a-Service hub. The island's value is jurisdictional (zero capital controls, stable legal system, accepted by UAE/KSA regulators as DR site). The main competition is "doing nothing" or using an on-premises DR site in the client's own country.
